## Article design
- Quick direct answer: protocols differ in device addressing, use cases and control requirements; not interchangeable by default.
- Matrix: typical control architecture, dimming scenes, dynamic architectural effects, interoperability caveats, design considerations.
- Office example and facade RGBW example (explicitly illustrative, not claims about a real client).
- Engineering checklist: driver compatibility, controllers, power and wiring, commissioning, maintenance.
- When hybrid/control gateways are needed; use up-to-date product manuals, avoid implying universal compatibility.
- Links to specific Nassli products actually supporting the protocols and to approved spec sheets.
- CTA: Ask engineer for protocol compatibility review.

**Required:** qualified technical author, external standards/manufacturer documentation and exact available product models.
**Do not publish** as a technical standard without review.
