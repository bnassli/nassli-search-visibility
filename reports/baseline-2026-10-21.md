# Live technical baseline — 21 October 2026

**Scope:** Read-only HTTP requests to the public site (8 configured pages, host variants, robots.txt and every sitemap it declares) with `python -m src.nassli_audit`, plus manual inspection of the returned HTML. No login, no admin access, no Search Console data. Supersedes the "not yet verified" list in `baseline-2026-10-20.md` where marked below.

## Confirmed stack (from public HTML generator tags, asset paths and response headers)

| Layer | Observed | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| CMS | WordPress 7.0.7 | `wp-sitemap.xml` is WordPress core sitemap → **no SEO plugin is active** |
| Shop | WooCommerce 11.2.0 | Product schema, prices and Add-to-cart come from Woo |
| Multilingual | WPML 4.6.11 (`/ar/` sub-directory, EN default) | Canonical/hreflang behavior is controlled here |
| Theme | Porto + `porto-child` | Product title rendered as `h2`; Porto builder templates are public post types |
| Builders | Elementor 4.3.5 + Elementor Pro, Slider Revolution 6.7.37 | Performance review needed (heavy pages ≈ 200–230 KB HTML) |
| Quote / forms | YITH WooCommerce Request a Quote Premium, Contact Form 7, Fluent Forms | Two form plugins → decide which one owns RFQ before tracking (SEO-010) |
| Other | Chaty (chat widget), YITH Wishlist, YITH Ajax Navigation, Customer Reviews for WooCommerce, 3D FlipBook | |
| Server | PHP 8.2, `Server: hcdn` header (appears to be Hostinger CDN — confirm with hosting account) | |

Host handling is correct: `http://`, `www.` and `http://www.` all 301 to `https://nassli-led.com.sa/`.

## Findings (ranked)

| # | Severity | Finding | Evidence | Fix owner / Issue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🔴 Critical | **Arabic pages canonicalize to English.** `/ar/`, `/ar/our-lighting/`, `/ar/product/led-up-light/` declare themselves as `hreflang="ar"` but `rel=canonical` points to the English URL. Google is told the Arabic page is a duplicate and may drop it from Arabic results. | audit: `canonical_conflicts_with_hreflang` | WPML/SEO config — SEO-021 |
| 2 | 🔴 Critical | **No SEO plugin:** no `meta description` on any audited page; site title is the bare domain (`nassli-led.com.sa`); homepage `og:title` = `front`. | audit: `missing_meta_description`, `generic_title`, `generic_og_title` | SEO-022 |
| 3 | 🔴 Critical | **Product schema publishes a false price and junk SKU.** LED UP LIGHT: `Offer` with `price: 0.00`, `sku: 1234567812-1-1-1-…`, `image` = `LED-LINEAR-45.jpg`. Quote-only products should not expose a zero-price Offer. Likely affects most of the 70 products. | audit: `schema_offer_price_zero`, `schema_sku_suspicious` | SEO-024 |
| 4 | 🟠 High | **Arabic pages are not translated in metadata/headings:** `/ar/products/` title and H1 are `Products`; `/ar/product/led-up-light/` title is `LED UP LIGHT – nassli-led.com.sa`. Arabic homepage H1 uses brand spelling **ناسلي**; owner-approved spelling is **نسلي**. | audit: `duplicate_title_across_pages` on EN/AR pairs | SEO-005, SEO-007 |
| 5 | 🟠 High | **Sitemap lists 28 Porto theme templates** (`/?porto_builder=header`, `single-product`, …) and a **user archive sitemap** (`wp-sitemap-users-1.xml`) that exposes a WordPress login name. | audit: `sitemap_lists_theme_templates`, `sitemap_exposes_user_archives` | SEO-023 |
| 6 | 🟠 High | **No H1** on `/our-lighting/` and product pages (Porto renders product title as `h2`). | audit: `missing_h1` | SEO-005 / theme child override |
| 7 | 🟡 Medium | `/products/` (EN and AR) has **no canonical** tag. | audit: `canonical_missing_or_multiple` | SEO-006 |
| 8 | 🟡 Medium | Images without `alt`: homepage 23 of 24, product page 13 of 19. | audit: `images_missing_alt_review` | SEO-016 |
| 9 | 🟡 Medium | Product page repeats the order-code table 3× in HTML and shows Add-to-cart next to Request-a-Quote. | manual HTML inspection | SEO-009 |
| 10 | 🟡 Medium | Generator tags publish exact WordPress/WooCommerce/WPML/Elementor versions. | manual | Security hygiene with SEO-022 |
