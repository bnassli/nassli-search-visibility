# Live technical baseline — 21 October 2026

**Scope:** Read-only HTTP requests to the public site (8 configured pages, host variants, robots.txt and every sitemap it declares) with `python -m src.nassli_audit`, plus manual inspection of the returned HTML. No login, no admin access, no Search Console data. Supersedes the "not yet verified" list in `baseline-2026-10-20.md` where marked below.

> **Revised 2026-10-21 after Codex review** (Issues #21–#24). Corrections are listed at the end; the table below already reflects them. Generator strings are public signals, not independently verified package versions.

## Findings (ranked)

| # | Severity | Finding | Evidence | Fix owner / Issue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🔴 Critical | **Translated Arabic pages canonicalize to English.** `/ar/` (92% Arabic in `<main>`) and `/ar/our-lighting/` (88%) declare `hreflang="ar"` but `rel=canonical` points to the English URL, telling Google the translation is a duplicate. This shows conflicting signals, not proof of Google's chosen canonical (needs Search Console). `/ar/product/led-up-light/` is different: only 3% Arabic, i.e. an untranslated WPML fallback, where canonical-to-original is expected until the product is translated (#7/#12). | audit: `canonical_conflicts_with_hreflang` vs `canonical_to_original_on_untranslated_review` | SEO-021 |
| 2 | 🔴 Critical | **No SEO plugin output:** no `meta description` on any audited page; site title is the bare domain (`nassli-led.com.sa`); homepage `og:title` = `front`. | audit: `missing_meta_description`, `generic_title`, `generic_og_title` | SEO-022 |
| 3 | 🔴 Critical | **Product schema makes unsupported commercial claims on quote-only pages.** Sampled LED UP LIGHT (EN/AR), LED LINEAR, LED LINEAR RS: `Offer` with `price: 0.00`, `priceCurrency: USD`, `availability: InStock`, validity to 2027-12-31, and placeholder `sku: 1234567812-1-1-…`, while the page shows a quote flow and real `NSL-…` order codes. Full 70-product coverage not yet audited. | audit: `schema_offer_on_quote_only_page`, `schema_offer_price_zero`, `schema_sku_placeholder` | SEO-024 |
| 4 | 🟠 High | **Arabic pages are not translated in metadata/headings:** `/ar/products/` title and H1 are `Products`; `/ar/product/led-up-light/` title and body are still English (3% Arabic). Arabic homepage H1 uses brand spelling **ناسلي**; owner-approved spelling is **نسلي**. | audit: `duplicate_title_across_pages` on EN/AR pairs | SEO-005, SEO-007 |
| 5 | 🟠 High | **Sitemap lists 28 Porto theme templates** (`/?porto_builder=header`, `single-product`, …) and a **user sitemap** (`wp-sitemap-users-1.xml`) exposing one public author-archive slug (equality to a login name not established; slug deliberately not recorded here). Zero `/ar/` URLs and zero hreflang entries across the whole tree. Removing URLs from a sitemap does not deindex them. | audit: `sitemap_lists_theme_templates`, `sitemap_exposes_user_archives`, `sitemap_has_no_arabic_urls` | SEO-023 |
| 6 | 🟠 High | **No H1** on `/our-lighting/` and product pages (Porto renders product title as `h2`). | audit: `missing_h1` | SEO-005 / theme child override |
| 7 | 🟡 Medium | `/products/` (EN and AR) has **no canonical** tag. | audit: `canonical_missing` | SEO-006 |
| 8 | 🟡 Medium | Images without `alt`: homepage 23 of 24, product page 13 of 19. | audit: `images_missing_alt_review` | SEO-016 |
| 9 | 🟡 Medium | Product page repeats the order-code table 3× in HTML and shows Add-to-cart next to Request-a-Quote. | manual HTML inspection | SEO-009 |
| 10 | 🟡 Medium | Generator tags publish exact WordPress/WooCommerce/WPML/Elementor versions. | manual | Security hygiene with SEO-022 |
