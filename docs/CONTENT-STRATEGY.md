# Commercial page & editorial architecture

## Audience and intent
- **Design consultant**: luminaire specification, photometry, BIM/IES, DALI/DMX, compliance.
- **Electrical contractor**: availability, submittal package, quantities, installation instructions, lead time.
- **Procurement**: manufacturer status, project credentials, warranty, RFQ and supplier qualification.
- **Architect**: finishes, shapes, curves, visual quality, project showcase.

## Page hierarchy proposal (URLs pending CMS audit)
- Manufacturer landing: Saudi LED Lighting Manufacturer / مصنع إنارة LED بالسعودية.
- Product category pages: Architectural/Façade; Custom Linear; Office/Commercial; Industrial/High Bay; Outdoor/Landscape; Emergency.
- Product detail: permanent SKU, specs tables, dimensions, controls, IP/IK where verified, downloads, images and request quote.
- Solutions by sector: offices, healthcare, hospitality, retail, warehouse & industrial, streets & outdoors.
- Case studies: client approval, challenge, constraints, lighting solution, luminaires and objective data, original images.
- Knowledge base: DALI vs DMX, choosing IP, glare/UGR, linear fitting, uplight deployment, high-bay design.
- Factory/quality: manufacturing process, QC, testing only if documented and authorized.

## Editorial approval checklist
- Provide original insight and evidence; credited subject-matter expert.
- Verify numeric claims against current manufacturer datasheet and actual SKU.
- Use clear AR and EN independent copy, not literal keyword translations.
- Add unique photos/technical diagrams when owned and permitted.
- Link to product, category, case study and relevant quote form; no excessive exact-match anchor text.
- Cite authoritative standards accurately, reference latest editions when relevant and avoid saying certified without proof.
- Review publish date, author/title display, correct URL/canonical, and metadata. Record in tracking inventory.
